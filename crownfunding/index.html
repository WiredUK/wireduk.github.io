Frequently Asked Tooth Questions

Do you accept baby teeth? 🍼
Absolutely! Baby teeth are considered a delicacy on the collector's market. We pay a slight premium for first-ever teeth — sentimental value is real, but cash is realer.
What about wisdom teeth? 🧠
We love wisdom teeth. The existential suffering they caused only adds to the mystique. Removed by a professional dentist preferred; removed by a door and a piece of string — we don't judge.
Will this affect my dental insurance? 🦺
Technically no, legally we can't say, spiritually unclear. We recommend consulting your dentist, your accountant, and anyone who still has all their teeth.
What condition should my tooth be in? ✨
Clean and dry, please! Give it a little polish. Think of it as a tiny white gemstone that used to be in your face. First impressions matter even in the tooth trade.
How many teeth can I sell? 🤑
Adults have 32 teeth. We're not saying sell them all. We're just saying the math is right there. Please consult a physician before selling more than four.
